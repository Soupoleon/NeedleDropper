    Plays the real recording in your Spotify app. Spotify lets every player connect through a free developer app of their own: about two minutes, once.

    1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (opens in a new tab), sign in with the account you listen with and choose Create app. Any name will do; tick Web API.
    2. Under Redirect URIs add exactly this address, then Save: http://127.0.0.1:8321/callback
    3. Open your app's Settings, copy the Client ID, paste it here and sign in:

    Listening for World of Warcraft…

    Nothing happens?
    • Play in Windowed or Windowed (Fullscreen) mode: NeedleDropper reads a tiny strip in the top-left corner of the game window.
    • Check that NeedleDropper is ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen.
    • Installed the addon while the game was running? Restart World of Warcraft.
    • Still nothing? and look at needledropper.log.
