    Play through NeedleDropper's own player, or choose a separate Spotify desktop app below. The built-in player needs Google Chrome. Connect through a free Spotify developer app of your own: a one-time setup.

    Play music with
    Spotify, then YouTube (set in game: Options > AddOns > NeedleDropper)
    1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (opens in a new tab), sign in with the account you listen with and choose Create app. Any name will do; tick Web API and Web Playback SDK.
    2. Your developer app from NeedleDropper 1.2.0: open it in the Spotify developer dashboard (opens in a new tab), then Settings → Edit, tick Web Playback SDK and Save. (Its Redirect URI and Client ID stay as they are.)
    3. Under Redirect URIs add exactly this address, then Save: http://127.0.0.1:8321/callback

  3. 3

    YouTube

    Checking…

    Nothing to set up. While a song plays from YouTube, a small player window shows on top of the game in the corner you chose, and hides again when the song is over: YouTube's rules say the video must stay visible while it plays. It never takes the focus from the game. It needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

    Player box
    Top right (set in game: Options > AddOns > NeedleDropper)

    Optional: signed in with YouTube Premium, YouTube songs play without ads. The sign-in opens in the player box's own browser window, not your everyday browser.

    Listening for World of Warcraft…

    Nothing happens?
    • Play in Windowed or Fullscreen (Windowed) mode: NeedleDropper reads a tiny strip in the top-left corner of the game window.
    • Check that NeedleDropper is ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen.
    • Installed the addon while the game was running? Restart World of Warcraft.
    • Still nothing? and look at needledropper.log.
