# Azeroth Mixtape: liner notes

*The default NeedleDropper soundtrack*, built into the addon and turned on from the start
(`packs/azeroth-mixtape.json`).

In Death Stranding, Hideo Kojima saves his licensed songs for one moment: you crest a ridge after a long
walk, the land opens up below you, a song starts and its title card fades in. That moment is a needle
drop. NeedleDropper brings it to World of Warcraft: Forever. The first time a character arrives
somewhere, a song chosen for that place starts, and a title card shows the place and "Artist · Title".

*Azeroth Mixtape* is the soundtrack NeedleDropper starts with. It skips the quiet corners and goes
straight to the places every player remembers: all six capitals, every starting zone, the signature
zones, famous landmarks from Goldshire to Everlook, and the four new Forever zones. That makes
**38 needle drops** (25 zones and 13 landmarks) by 37 artists, about 2 hours 40 minutes of music.

The mix is half chills and half laughs, because Azeroth is both. You can stand on the rainy docks of
Menethil Harbor waiting for the boat and feel genuinely wistful, then walk into Goldshire. Some arrivals
deserve a song that fits the place so well it gives you goosebumps. Others have been community in-jokes
for more than twenty years, and the only honest soundtrack for them is the punchline. So
**18 drops are fitting and 20 are funny**, and every drop is tagged as one or the other.

The soundtrack holds metadata only: titles, artists, lengths and links, never audio and never lyrics.
The songs belong to their artists and labels. Every drop has its length and an official YouTube upload
that allows embedding, so with the NeedleDropper app running it plays without connecting any account.
If you connect Spotify Premium, the app plays the songs there instead, looking each one up by artist
and title (see [Music services](PROVIDERS.md)). A few of the YouTube uploads are blocked in some
countries; there, connect Spotify or paste another official upload for that place (see
[Make it yours](#make-it-yours)).

**Legend**

- ◆ **Fitting**: the place's own mood, epic or wistful. These are the chills.
- ★ **Funny**: a pun, a meme or a community in-joke. These are the laughs.

Landmarks name their zone in brackets. Lengths are minutes:seconds.

## Kalimdor

- ★ **Durotar** — Dolly Parton · *9 to 5* (2:45) — Your first job in the Valley of Trials is waking lazy peons, so Durotar opens with an anthem for the working orc.
- ◆ **Mulgore** — John Barry · *The John Dunbar Theme* (2:16) — Endless golden plains, wind in the tall grass and mesas on the horizon: a frontier theme fit for the tauren homeland.
- ★ **The Barrens** — Toto · *Africa* (4:55) — Lions, zhevra and endless golden savanna: Barrens chat finally gets the meme anthem it has always deserved.
- ★ **The Crossroads** (The Barrens) — U2 · *I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For* (4:37) — Every Horde hero at the Crossroads ends up asking Barrens chat where Mankrik's wife is. Bono hasn't found her either.
- ◆ **Teldrassil** — Enya · *May It Be* (3:31) — Moonlight on the World Tree and the most elven song in film history, for the night elves' first steps into the world.
- ★ **The Shimmering Flats** (Thousand Needles) — Elton John · *Rocket Man (I Think It's Going to Be a Long, Long Time)* (4:41) — Gnome and goblin rocket cars tear across the salt flats, and you're the one stuck collecting Rocket Car Parts.
- ★ **Tanaris** — Darude · *Sandstorm* (3:45) — An entire desert of blowing sand, and the one song that is basically required by law to play there.
- ★ **Gadgetzan** (Tanaris) — ABBA · *Money, Money, Money* (3:06) — Steamwheedle goblins sell to Horde and Alliance alike, as long as the gold is real and the auction cut is theirs.
- ★ **Felwood** — Britney Spears · *Toxic* (3:18) — Fel-poisoned rivers, oozing slimes and a forest rotten to the roots: Felwood is literally toxic. Britney said it first.
- ◆ **Un'Goro Crater** — John Williams · *Theme from Jurassic Park* (3:27) — A lost crater of dinosaurs, steaming springs and glowing crystals. There's only one way a film would score this arrival.
- ◆ **Silithus** — Kansas · *Dust in the Wind* (3:26) — Wind-scoured dunes over a buried Qiraji empire, where whole armies of the Ahn'Qiraj war effort turned to sand.
- ★ **Everlook** (Winterspring) — Vanilla Ice · *Ice Ice Baby* (4:31) — The coldest town in vanilla WoW gets the most vanilla Ice of all.
- ◆ **Orgrimmar** — Hans Zimmer & Lisa Gerrard · *Now We Are Free* (4:14) — Thrall fought as a gladiator slave, then freed the orcs from the camps. His city gets Gladiator's anthem of freedom.
- ★ **Thunder Bluff** — Tom Petty · *Free Fallin'* (4:15) — For everyone who ever stepped off a Thunder Bluff elevator one second too early.
- ★ **Darnassus** — Céline Dion · *All by Myself* (5:10) — Azeroth's loveliest capital and famously its emptiest: the bank, the auction house and the moonwell are all yours.
- ◆ **Mount Hyjal** — Queen · *Who Wants to Live Forever* (5:16) — Night elves gave up their immortality on this mountain to stop Archimonde, and now the whole game is called Forever.
- ◆ **Shen'dralas** — Coldplay · *Viva la Vida* (4:01) — Highborne exiles still dressed for an empire that fell ten thousand years ago, scored with a deposed king's lament.

## Eastern Kingdoms

- ★ **The Dark Portal** (Blasted Lands) — AC/DC · *Highway to Hell* (3:28) — On the far side of the portal lies Hellfire Peninsula, and AC/DC named the road there back in 1979.
- ★ **Tirisfal Glades** — Evanescence · *Bring Me to Life* (3:55) — The Forsaken begin by waking up dead in Deathknell, so Tirisfal gets the internet's most famous plea to be woken up.
- ◆ **Light's Hope Chapel** (Eastern Plaguelands) — Jeff Buckley · *Hallelujah* (6:53) — The last holy ground in the Plaguelands, where the Argent Dawn still kneel: arrive, breathe and let it play.
- ★ **Southshore** (Hillsbrad Foothills) — Edwin Starr · *War* (3:20) — Southshore vs. Tarren Mill raged for months before battlegrounds and earned exactly what this song says war is good for.
- ◆ **Tarren Mill** (Hillsbrad Foothills) — Dire Straits · *Brothers in Arms* (4:58) — Tarren Mill vs. Southshore, vanilla's endless world-PvP war, scored by rock's most mournful soldiers' lament. Timed to the 4:58 single edit.
- ◆ **Aerie Peak** (The Hinterlands) — John Powell · *Test Drive* (2:36) — Wildhammer gryphons and Celtic fiddles: How to Train Your Dragon's first-flight cue was all but written for this cliff.
- ◆ **Dun Morogh** — Richard Armitage & The Dwarf Cast · *Misty Mountains* (1:43) — Deep dwarven voices mourning a lost mountain home: written for Erebor, felt by every gnome who fled Gnomeregan.
- ★ **Burning Steppes** — Billy Joel · *We Didn't Start the Fire* (4:51) — The Dark Irons summoned Ragnaros and set this whole land ablaze. Ask any of them, though, and it wasn't their fault.
- ◆ **Elwynn Forest** — The Beatles · *Here Comes the Sun* (3:05) — Dawn over Northshire Abbey and a whole world ahead: the gentlest possible start to an adventure.
- ★ **Goldshire** (Elwynn Forest) — Eagles · *Hotel California* (6:31) — Azeroth's most notorious inn since 2004. The Lion's Pride never closes, and its regulars have been here since launch.
- ★ **Karazhan** (Deadwind Pass) — Queen · *Bohemian Rhapsody* (5:54) — Medivh's tower: a possessed wizard, a haunted ballroom and its own opera house. Only rock's most operatic epic will do.
- ★ **Duskwood** — Michael Jackson · *Thriller* (5:57) — Worgen in the woods, ghouls at Raven Hill and Stitches on the road: Duskwood is basically the music video.
- ★ **Stranglethorn Vale** — Guns N' Roses · *Welcome to the Jungle* (4:33) — Raptors, tigers, trolls and a gank behind every tree: Stranglethorn is exactly the jungle Axl warned you about.
- ◆ **Booty Bay** (Stranglethorn Vale) — Klaus Badelt · *He's a Pirate* (1:30) — Walk through the jaws of a giant shark into a goblin pirate harbour, and only one cue can possibly be playing.
- ★ **Westfall** — Adele · *Skyfall* (4:46) — Skyfall, but make it Westfall: a Bond-sized ballad for broken windmills and a Defias Brotherhood plotting its return.
- ◆ **Menethil Harbor** (Wetlands) — Otis Redding · *(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay* (2:43) — Rain, gulls and the long wait for the boat: every Alliance player has spent a small lifetime on these docks.
- ◆ **Stormwind City** — David Bowie · *"Heroes"* (6:11) — You walk in beneath the giant champion statues of the Valley of Heroes, and Bowie's anthem names the place for you.
- ★ **Ironforge** — Wind Rose · *Diggy Diggy Hole* (5:15) — A whole city carved out of a mountain by dwarves who have never once stopped digging. This is their work song.
- ◆ **Undercity** — Siouxsie and the Banshees · *Cities in Dust* (4:03) — The Banshee Queen rules a city beneath the ashes of Lordaeron, so naturally the Banshees supply the anthem.
- ◆ **Riverglades** — Leon Bridges · *River* (3:58) — A brand-new land of rivers and reeds, welcomed with a hushed, gospel-quiet hymn about being baptised in the water.

## Make it yours

*Azeroth Mixtape* is built in and read-only, but every song in it is yours to change, in game, in a
couple of clicks. Type `/needle` to open the NeedleDropper window; the **Drops** tab lists every place
and its song.

1. **Find the place.** Search for it (*Goldshire*, *Toto*), or simply go there: the window opens on
   the drop for where you stand. To see only this soundtrack, pick *Azeroth Mixtape* under
   **Showing:**.
2. **Change the song.** Click **Change song here**. NeedleDropper copies the drop into **My Drops**, a
   soundtrack of your own that sits at the top of your list, so your version is the one that plays.
   The cursor lands in **Artist** with the old name selected: type the new artist and title, and
   paste a Spotify or YouTube link if you have one. Press **Test** to hear it at once.
3. **Or silence it.** Some places are better quiet: **Turn off here**.
4. **Changed your mind?** **Revert to Azeroth Mixtape** brings the original song back.
5. **Add places it doesn't cover.** Stand there (a town, an inn, a bridge, a dungeon: anywhere) and
   press **Add drop here**.

Your changes live in My Drops, so when an addon update fixes a Mixtape song, the fix still reaches
every place you didn't change. If you would rather rework the whole thing, **Duplicate** on the
**Soundtracks** tab gives you *Azeroth Mixtape (my copy)* to edit from top to bottom.

Want a song in every zone? Turn on *Azeroth Unbound* as well (the quiet, Kojima-style score for every
zone; see its [liner notes](SOUNDTRACK.md)) and keep *Azeroth Mixtape* above it on the Soundtracks
tab: when two soundtracks have a song for the same place, the one higher in the list plays. The
Mixtape then plays at its 38 places and Unbound scores everywhere else.

To share your version, select My Drops on the Soundtracks tab and click **Share...**: you get a code
to paste into Discord, and friends paste it into **Import...**. To hear a drop without waiting for a
first visit, press **Test** next to it in the window, or type `/needle play Goldshire`. Everything
else, including the file format for soundtrack authors, is in [Soundtracks](PACKS.md).
